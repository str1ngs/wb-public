The UGREEN Nexode 45W Charger features a built-in 0.7m retractable fast-charging cable, eliminating the need to pack loose wires. Powered by GaNInfinity technology and equipped with 3 total ports, it delivers professional 45W charging (supporting Samsung Super Fast Charging 2.0) while maintaining an ultra-compact footprint ideal for travel and everyday carry.

Who it's for

Travelers, commuters, and minimalists who want an all-in-one wall charger with an integrated pull-and-retract cable to charge phones, tablets, and smaller laptops without cable clutter.

Strengths

  • Built-in 0.7m retractable fast-charging cable eliminates tangles and removes the need for extra wires
  • 3-in-1 multi-port design allows simultaneous charging of a phone, tablet, and accessories from a single wall outlet
  • Supports 45W professional charging and Samsung Super Fast Charging 2.0 (e.g., Galaxy S25 Ultra to 67% in 30 minutes)
  • Heavy-duty protection, travel-friendly

Trade-offs

  • Integrated cable cannot be easily replaced independently if physically damaged

Charging and expansion

Utilizing GaNInfinity technology, the charger maximizes power conversion efficiency to 95% while suppressing heat. The built-in 69cm pull-out cable provides convenient plug-and-play charging, backed by multi-protocol compatibility including Samsung SFC 2.0 and standard USB-PD.

Frequently asked questions

How does the built-in cable work?

It features an integrated 0.7-meter pull-and-retract fast-charging cable that extends smoothly for use and rolls back inside for neat, tangle-free storage.

Can the charger power multiple devices at once?

Yes, its 3-in-1 design allows you to plug in and charge your phone, tablet, and headphones simultaneously from a single compact wall block.

Does it support fast charging for Samsung Galaxy devices?

Yes, it supports Samsung Super Fast Charging 2.0, capable of charging models like the Galaxy S25 Ultra from 0% up to 67% in approximately 30 minutes.
