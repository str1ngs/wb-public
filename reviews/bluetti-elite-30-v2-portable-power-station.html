The BLUETTI Elite 30 V2 is a compact 288Wh portable power station featuring a long-lasting LFP battery, 600W rated power (1,500W lifting power), and a lightning-fast <=10ms pro-grade UPS backup. Equipped with UltraCell energy-efficient technology and 140W USB-C PD fast charging, it recharges to 80% in just 45 minutes.

Who it's for

Campers, remote workers, and apartment dwellers looking for a lightweight, silent, and ultra-reliable backup power source with rapid recharging.

Strengths

  • Rapid TurboBoost charging gets you to 80% capacity in just 45 minutes
  • Pro-grade <=10ms UPS switch time with 980W bypass power protects sensitive electronics
  • Long-lasting LiFePO4 battery delivers over 3,000 life cycles and a 10-year lifespan
  • Powerful 140W USB-C port for fast laptop and mobile device charging

Trade-offs

  • 288Wh capacity is optimized for portable essentials rather than heavy appliances
  • Solar input limited to 200W max (12V-28V, 10A)

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 980W max AC input for rapid recharging and up to 200W solar input via 12V-28V ports.
