Who it's for

Ideal for digital nomads, professionals, and travelers who need a single, pocket-friendly charging brick capable of driving a modern USB-C laptop alongside a phone and tablet simultaneously without cluttering their everyday carry bag.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Anker Nano 65W fast-charge a MacBook Pro?

Yes. Each individual USB-C port delivers up to 65W max, which is enough to charge a 14-inch MacBook Pro from 0% to 50% in roughly 35 minutes.

How many devices can I plug in at once?

You can plug in up to three devices simultaneously across its multi-port configuration (dual USB-C and USB-A layout), allowing you to power your laptop, tablet, and phone with a single wall outlet.

Does it run hot during fast charging?

Thanks to ActiveShield 4.0 thermal monitoring and TÜV Rheinland certification, surface temperatures run notably cooler than international safety benchmarks.
