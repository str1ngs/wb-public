The Anker Nano Charger (65W, 3 Ports) punches well above its weight class, delivering a true 65W max output from its individual USB-C ports—enough to fast-charge a 14-inch MacBook Pro from 0 to 50% in 35 minutes. Built with GaN II technology, it packs multi-device capability into a footprint 41% smaller than standard 65W chargers.

Who it's for

Ideal for digital nomads, professionals, and travelers who need a single, pocket-friendly charging brick capable of driving a modern USB-C laptop alongside a phone and tablet simultaneously without cluttering their everyday carry bag.

Strengths

  • True 65W output from a single port handles modern MacBooks with ease
  • 41% smaller than standard 65W blocks with a highly travel-friendly, pocket-sized form factor
  • ActiveShield 4.0 keeps operating temperatures significantly lower under load
  • Three ports allow simultaneous multi-device charging on the go
  • Textured shell and premium metal frame offer a clean, durable aesthetic

Trade-offs

  • Total power output is shared intelligently when multiple ports are occupied
  • Does not include a foldable plug variant in all regional versions
  • Premium price relative to basic single-port power blocks
  • USB-A port has only 22.5W output

Charging and expansion

Powered by advanced GaN II technology, each USB-C port is engineered to push up to 65W max independently. When charging multiple devices at once, the internal power allocation dynamically shifts to optimize speeds across connected gear while keeping thermal metrics safe via ActiveShield 4.0.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Anker Nano 65W fast-charge a MacBook Pro?

Yes. Each individual USB-C port delivers up to 65W max, which is enough to charge a 14-inch MacBook Pro from 0% to 50% in roughly 35 minutes.

How many devices can I plug in at once?

You can plug in up to three devices simultaneously across its multi-port configuration (dual USB-C and USB-A layout), allowing you to power your laptop, tablet, and phone with a single wall outlet.

Does it run hot during fast charging?

Thanks to ActiveShield 4.0 thermal monitoring and TÜV Rheinland certification, surface temperatures run notably cooler than international safety benchmarks.
