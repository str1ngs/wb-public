The Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 packs 1,024Wh of LiFePO4 capacity and a robust 2,000W output (3,000W peak) into a footprint 14% smaller and 11% lighter than comparable models. Featuring HyperFlash technology for a 49-minute ultra-fast AC recharge and smart app controls with Time-of-Use (TOU) mode, it is built to serve as a reliable, long-lasting power source.

Who it's for

Ideal for campers, van-lifers, and homeowners looking for a compact, fast-charging emergency power source to keep essential appliances, laptops, and medical gear running during grid failures.

Strengths

  • Ultra-fast 1,600W AC recharging brings the unit to 100% in just 49 minutes
  • Robust 2,000W output (3,000W peak) across 9 versatile ports
  • Long-lasting InfiniPower LiFePO4 battery rated for 4,000 cycles to 80% capacity
  • Fast 1.8-hour solar recharging capability with up to 600W input
  • Under 10 ms UPS switchover ensures seamless emergency power for sensitive devices

Trade-offs

  • Base capacity of 1,024Wh requires solar or grid access for extended multi-day outages
  • Heavier than ultra-light backpack power banks, though optimized for its power class
  • Kinda expensive for now

Charging and expansion

Equipped with upgraded HyperFlash technology, the C1000 Gen 2 pulls up to 1,600W from an AC wall outlet to fully recharge in 49 minutes when enabled via the Anker app. It also supports up to 600W (60V max) of solar input for a full off-grid recharge in about 1.8 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 recharge?

Using AC wall power with HyperFlash enabled, it recharges from 0% to 100% in just 49 minutes at 1,600W. Solar recharging takes about 1.8 hours with a 600W input.

Does it support UPS functionality?

Yes, it features a sub-10 ms UPS switchover time, making it reliable for keeping critical electronics like laptops and medical equipment running without interruption during a blackout.

What is the lifespan of the battery?

The unit is built with InfiniPower technology and rated for 4,000 battery cycles while retaining at least 80% of its original capacity.
