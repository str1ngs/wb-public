The BLUETTI Elite 300 is a Frost & Sullivan-certified ultra-compact 3kWh portable power station packing 3,014.4Wh of capacity and 2,400W of continuous AC output (4,800W peak) into a remarkably small footprint. Featuring an RV-ready 12V/30A DC port, dual high-wattage USB-C ports (up to 140W), 1,200W solar input, a 10ms UPS mode, and Bluetooth/Wi-Fi app control, it delivers robust off-grid and emergency power.

Who it's for

Homeowners seeking reliable emergency backup, RV travelers, and off-grid enthusiasts who need massive multi-kilowatt energy storage and heavy appliance support in a space-saving design.

Strengths

  • 3,014.4Wh capacity and 2,400W continuous output (4,800W peak) handles high-draw appliances like microwaves and heaters
  • Ultra-durable LiFePO4 cells offer an exceptional 6,000+ charge cycles to 80% capacity
  • Dedicated RV-ready 12V/30A DC port provides stable high-amperage power for portable refrigerators and camper setups
  • Blazing-fast charging support with up to 2,300W AC input and 1,200W solar input
  • Integrated 10ms UPS mode ensures seamless power backup for critical home electronics during blackouts
  • Comprehensive connectivity including 140W USB-C, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi app management

Trade-offs

  • Weighs 26.3 kg, making it a quite heavy stationary unit
  • More for home backup rather than lightweight backpacking

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 2,300W AC input for rapid wall charging, alongside a 1,200W solar/DC input (12V–60V, 22A) that can fully recharge the unit in about 4.1 hours via sunlight.

Frequently asked questions

What appliances can the BLUETTI Elite 300 power?

With 2,400W output (4,800W peak), it can easily run heavy-duty resistive and inductive loads such as refrigerators, microwaves, coffee makers, electric kettles, and power tools.

How does the UPS function operate during a power outage?

It switches to battery power within 10 milliseconds, keeping sensitive devices like desktop computers, routers, and medical equipment running without interruption.

Can the Elite 300 be charged with solar panels?

Yes, it accepts up to 1,200W of solar input (12V–60V, 22A), allowing for a full solar recharge in approximately 4.1 hours under optimal conditions.
