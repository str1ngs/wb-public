The EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro is a highly portable 768Wh power station featuring a long-lasting LFP battery rated for 3,000 cycles. Delivering 800W of rated power (expandable to 1,600W with X-Boost), it boasts ultra-fast AC recharging from 0% to 100% in just 70 minutes and a lightweight 7.8 kg build.

Who it's for

Campers, outdoor enthusiasts, and mobile workers looking for a lightweight, fast-charging, and reliable medium-capacity portable power station.

Strengths

  • Extremely fast AC recharging reaches 100% in just 70 minutes (940W max input)
  • Long-lasting LFP battery provides 3,000 cycles to 80%+ capacity for a 10-year lifespan
  • Lightweight and compact at only 7.8 kg for easy transport
  • 800W output handles 80% of household appliances, with X-Boost up to 1,600W
  • TÜV Rheinland safety certified for ultimate peace of mind

Trade-offs

  • Not expandable with extra battery modules
  • Solar input limited to 220W max (11-50V, 13A)

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 940W AC fast charging (0-100% in 70 min), 220W solar input, 100W car charging, and 100W USB-C input/output.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does the RIVER 2 Pro recharge?

Using AC fast charging, it recharges from 0% to 100% in just 70 minutes.

What is the battery lifespan and chemistry?

It uses an LFP (LiFePO4) battery offering 3,000 cycles to 80%+ capacity, translating to a 10-year lifespan.

Can the capacity be expanded?

No, the RIVER 2 Pro has a fixed 768Wh capacity and is not expandable with extra batteries.
