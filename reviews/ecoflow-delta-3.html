The EcoFlow DELTA 3 is a powerful 1024Wh portable power station featuring ultra-fast X-Stream AC charging that hits 100% in just 56 minutes. With 1800W continuous output (up to 2400W/3600W via X-Boost), 11 versatile ports, whisper-quiet 30dB operation, and expandability up to 5 kWh using compatible extra batteries, it delivers robust off-grid and backup energy.

Who it's for

Homeowners seeking reliable emergency backup, campers, and outdoor enthusiasts who need high continuous AC output, fast turnaround charging options, and modular expandability.

Strengths

  • X-Stream AC charging goes from 0 to 70% in 30 minutes and 100% in 56 minutes via a 1500W input
  • High 1800W continuous output (2400W / 3600W peak via X-Boost) powers 99% of household appliances
  • Modular expandability up to 5 kWh using Delta Pro 3, Delta 2 Max, or Delta 2 extra batteries
  • Quiet 30dB operation under loads below 600W won't disturb sleep in tents or bedrooms
  • Easy to carry

Trade-offs

  • 1024Wh might not be enough for running powerful devices long time
  • Requires a high-wattage AC wall connection to achieve the maximum 56-minute recharge speed
  • Extra batteries required to scale capacity up to the 5 kWh maximum limit

Charging and expansion

Supports 5 distinct quick-charging methods including 1500W AC input (0-100% in 56 min), 500W MPPT solar charging (full in ~2 hours), an 800W battery charger option, Smart Generator integration, and multi-charging configurations.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does the EcoFlow DELTA 3 recharge?

Using the 1500W X-Stream AC input, it recharges from 0% to 70% in just 30 minutes, and reaches 100% in 56 minutes.

Can the capacity of the DELTA 3 be expanded?

Yes, it is compatible with DELTA 3, DELTA Pro 3, DELTA 2 Max, and DELTA 2 extra batteries, allowing you to scale total capacity up to 5 kWh.

What devices can it power?

With 1800W of continuous power (and up to 2400W/3600W via X-Boost) across 11 ports, it can run 99% of standard household appliances, heavy-duty electronics, and emergency gear.
