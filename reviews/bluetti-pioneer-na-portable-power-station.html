The BLUETTI Pioneer Na is an interesting portable power station which is utilizing sodium-ion chemistry to deliver performance in freezing temperatures down to -25°C. Featuring a 900Wh capacity, 1,500W AC output (2,250W lifting power), and fast dual AC and solar charging that reaches 80% in just 35 minutes, it offers robust and reliable off-grid power.

Who it's for

Outdoor enthusiasts, winter campers, and users living in cold climates who need a reliable, temperature-resilient power station that charges safely in sub-zero environments.

Strengths

  • Exceptional cold-weather performance, charging safely at -15°C and discharging down to -25°C
  • Blazing-fast dual AC and solar charging reaches 80% capacity in just 35 minutes
  • Advanced sodium-ion chemistry offers over 4,000 cycles and high thermal stability
  • Provides 1,500W continuous output (2,250W lifting power) to run essential appliances and tools
  • Extremely low standby loss ensures the battery holds its charge for months when idle

Trade-offs

  • Capacity is limited to 900Wh relative to its 16 kg physical weight
  • Requires regular maintenance cycling (every 3 months) during long-term storage
  • Sodium-ion power stations are yet not tested enough

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 1,400W AC input (reaching 80% in 45 minutes) and up to 1,900W combined AC and solar input to hit 80% in just 35 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

How well does it perform in freezing temperatures?

Unlike traditional lithium batteries, the sodium-ion chemistry allows it to charge safely at temperatures down to -15°C and power devices down to -25°C.

How fast does it recharge?

Using combined AC and solar input, it can refuel to 80% capacity in just 35 minutes, or via AC alone in about 45 minutes.

What is the lifespan of a sodium-ion power station?

The Pioneer Na is built for longevity, offering over 4,000 charge cycles before reaching 70% original capacity.
