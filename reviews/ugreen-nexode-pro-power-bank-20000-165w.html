The UGREEN Nexode Pro Power Bank (20,000mAh, 165W) features an innovative 65cm built-in roll-up cable, eliminating the need to carry separate wires. Delivering up to 165W total output across multiple ports (100W + 65W simultaneous dual-laptop charging), it combines high capacity with a sharp TFT display and robot status icons.

Who it's for

Digital nomads, remote workers, and frequent travelers who need to juice up two laptops simultaneously and appreciate integrated cable management for a clutter-free bag.

Strengths

  • Built-in 65cm bi-directional roll-up cable with 5 locking length settings for clean portability
  • Dual laptop charging support combining 100W and 65W simultaneously (165W total max output)
  • Ultra-fast 100W input recharging gets the power bank back to 100% in just 1.9 hours
  • High 20,000mAh capacity provides multiple full charges for phones, tablets, and lightweight laptops
  • Clear TFT display featuring intuitive real-time metrics and friendly robot icons

Trade-offs

  • Charging speed is intentionally throttled during the first 10 minutes to protect battery health
  • Built-in cable is fixed, meaning replacement requires unit service if damaged
  • Lower total watt-hours compared to heavier airline-max power banks

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 100W ultra-fast input power via compatible chargers, refilling the 20,000mAh internal battery in 1.9 hours while allowing pass-through charging. To protect cell health under heavy load, initial charging speeds are softly regulated for the first 10 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Can the UGREEN Nexode Pro charge two laptops at once?

Yes. It can simultaneously deliver up to 100W and 65W across its ports, making it fully capable of driving two demanding laptops at the same time.

How does the built-in cable work?

It features a 65cm bi-directional pull-and-retract roll-up cable with 5 adjustable length settings, keeping your workspace and travel bag free of loose wire tangles.

How long does it take to recharge the power bank?

When connected to a 100W fast wall charger, it recharges from 0% to 100% in approximately 1.9 hours.
