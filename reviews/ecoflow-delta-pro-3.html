The DELTA Pro 3 packs 4,096Wh of LFP capacity and a 4,000W continuous, dual-voltage inverter into a single wheeled chassis — and backs it with the fastest full recharge of any unit in this class, using EcoFlow's X-Stream fast-charging technology.

Who it's for

Built for homeowners and RV owners who need serious whole-home backup and don't want to wait around for it to charge. If a storm warning gives you two hours' notice, this is the unit that can go from empty to full before the power actually goes out.

Strengths

  • 0–80% AC charge in about 50 minutes — among the fastest in its capacity class
  • 120V/240V dual-voltage output handles central AC, dryers, and well pumps directly
  • Expandable to 48kWh with EcoFlow's smart extra batteries and Smart Generator
  • 10ms UPS switchover protects sensitive electronics during outages
  • Backward compatible with original DELTA Pro batteries and accessories

Trade-offs

  • At 113.5 lb, this is a two-person lift for stairs or a truck bed
  • Premium pricing — nearly double a capable 2kWh unit from a competing brand
  • Overkill for casual camping; the value case is home backup or RV, not weekend trips

Charging and expansion

AC input reaches 3,600W at 240V (1,800W at 120V), and solar input tops out at 2,600W. Combined AC + solar dual charging can hit roughly 7,000W, which is how EcoFlow gets a 0–80% charge down to about 50 minutes. A full 0–100% charge typically takes 1.5–2 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does the EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 charge?

Using AC wall power, it reaches 80% in roughly 50 minutes and a full charge in about 1.5 to 2 hours — several times faster than most 4kWh-class competitors.

Can the DELTA Pro 3 run a central air conditioner?

EcoFlow states it can power a 3-ton central AC unit or similar heavy 240V loads, thanks to its 4,000W continuous / 8,000W surge dual-voltage inverter.

Is the DELTA Pro 3 expandable?

Yes — with EcoFlow's smart extra batteries and Smart Generator, a single system can scale up to 48kWh for multi-day whole-home backup.
