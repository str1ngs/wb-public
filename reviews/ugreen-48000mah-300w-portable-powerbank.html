Who it's for

Campers, digital nomads, and outdoor enthusiasts who need massive portable battery capacity to charge laptops, tablets, and phones simultaneously far from any wall outlet.

Strengths

  • Massive 48,000mAh (153.6Wh) capacity easily powers multiple devices across multiple days
  • Supports 140W PD 3.1 bidirectional fast charging, fully refueling the power bank in just 1.5 hours
  • Versatile 5-port configuration (3 USB-C, 2 USB-A) with up to 300W total output
  • Long-lasting LiFePO4 battery chemistry retains over 80% capacity after 3,000 charge cycles
