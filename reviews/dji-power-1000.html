The DJI Power 1000 brings DJI's renowned engineering precision to the portable power station market. Packing 1,024Wh of LiFePO4 battery capacity and a robust 2,200W output, it is built to handle heavy-duty outdoor gear, rapid drone battery charging, and essential home appliances during a short power outage.

Who it's for

This unit is ideal for photographers, videographers, drone pilots, and campers who need reliable, fast-recharging power off-grid. It's also well-suited for urban apartment dwellers looking for a compact emergency backup for a refrigerator, router, and lights.

Strengths

  • Ultra-fast charging from 0% to 100% in roughly 70 minutes via AC wall power
  • Exceptional battery lifecycle of 4,000 cycles to 70% capacity
  • Dedicated DJI drone charging integration with fast-charge accessories
  • Dual USB-C ports supporting up to 140W Power Delivery (PD 3.1)
  • Quiet operation under heavy load compared to traditional gas generators

Trade-offs

  • Moderate base capacity (1,024Wh) requires careful load management for multi-day trips
  • Heavier than ultra-portable units, though still manageable to carry
  • Expansion options are more limited compared to heavy whole-home ecosystems

Charging and expansion

The DJI Power 1000 supports fast AC charging up to 1,200W, getting you back to full power in just over an hour. For solar enthusiasts, it accepts up to 800W of solar input, allowing clean off-grid recharges in favorable weather conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Can the DJI Power 1000 charge DJI drone batteries quickly?

Yes, DJI offers dedicated fast-charging cables and accessories that integrate directly with the Power 1000, enabling rapid charging for drone flight batteries.

How long will the DJI Power 1000 run household appliances?

With a 1,024Wh capacity and 2,200W output, it can power standard devices like laptops, lights, routers, and small refrigerators for several hours depending on the wattage draw.

What type of battery does the DJI Power 1000 use?

It utilizes safe and long-lasting LiFePO4 (LFP) battery chemistry rated for 4,000 cycles before dropping to 70% capacity.
