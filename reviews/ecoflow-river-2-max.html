The EcoFlow RIVER 2 Max is a portable 512Wh power station weighing just 6.1 kg with 60-minute AC recharge capability. Equipped with a long-lasting LFP battery rated for over 3,000 cycles, 500W continuous output (boostable to 1000W), and comprehensive app control, it delivers reliable off-grid power.

Who it's for

Campers, outdoor photographers, and travelers who need a lightweight, fast-charging power station to run essential appliances and mobile gear on the go.

Strengths

  • Blazing-fast AC recharge from 0 to 100% in just 60 minutes
  • Durable LFP battery chemistry provides 3,000+ cycles to 80% capacity (approx. 10 years of use)
  • Lightweight and portable at approximately 6.1 kg with compact dimensions
  • X-Boost mode scales output up to 1000W to run up to 80% of essential appliances
  • Versatile 4-way charging options including AC, car, solar (up to 220W), and 100W USB-C

Trade-offs

  • 512Wh capacity is optimized for portability rather than running heavy power-hungry home appliances for long periods
  • Maximum continuous AC output is limited to 500W without X-Boost
  • Requires access to a strong AC connection to achieve the 60-minute fast charge

Charging and expansion

Supports four charging methods: up to 660W max AC input for a full charge in 60 minutes, 220W max solar input (11-50V 13A), 100W car charging, and 100W USB-C input/output.

Frequently asked questions

How fast does the EcoFlow RIVER 2 Max recharge?

Using an AC wall outlet, it recharges from 0% to 100% in just 60 minutes.

What is the lifespan of the battery?

It utilizes safe LFP (LiFePO4) battery cells rated for over 3,000 cycles while retaining 80%+ capacity, translating to roughly 10 years of regular use.

Can it power high-wattage appliances?

It has a 500W continuous output rating, but its X-Boost mode can scale output up to 1000W to successfully run many essential heating or motor-driven appliances.
