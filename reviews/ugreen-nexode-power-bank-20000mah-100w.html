The UGREEN Nexode Power Bank 20000mAh 100W provides rapid laptop charging via its 100W USB-C1 port, a 20,000mAh capacity suitable for airline travel, and a 3-in-1 multi-port layout equipped with a digital LED status screen.

Who it's for

Travelers, remote workers, and students who need high-wattage USB-C power to charge laptops, tablets, and smartphones simultaneously while on the move.

Strengths

  • Powerful 100W USB-C output capable of fast-charging demanding laptops like the MacBook Pro
  • Airline-friendly 20,000mAh (72Wh) capacity that fits easily into travel bags
  • Equipped with 3 ports (2x USB-C, 1x USB-A) to charge up to three devices at once
  • Supports 65W rapid two-way recharging via USB-C1
  • Digital LED indicator provides clear battery percentage status at a glance

Trade-offs

  • Does not feature wireless charging or a detailed real-time TFT power telemetry display
  • Slightly bulky form factor due to the large capacity battery cells

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 65W input via USB-C1 for fast self-recharging in approximately 2 hours. Delivers up to 100W on USB-C1, 30W on USB-C2, and 22.5W on USB-A.

Frequently asked questions

Can I charge a laptop and a phone at the same time?

Yes, the 3-in-1 port layout allows you to charge up to three devices simultaneously across its USB-C and USB-A connections.
