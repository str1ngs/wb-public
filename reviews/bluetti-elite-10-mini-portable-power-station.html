The BLUETTI Elite 10 Mini packs a 128Wh LiFePO4 battery and a 200W AC outlet (300W peak) into an ultra-lightweight 1.8 kg handheld chassis. Featuring a 10ms UPS mode, 150W turbo AC recharging (70 mins to full), 100W USB-C fast charging, solar/car input options, and smart Bluetooth app control, it bridges the gap between high-capacity power banks and compact generators.

Who it's for

Campers, remote workers, photographers, and homeowners looking for an airline-friendly, long-lasting mini power station that can double as an emergency uninterruptible power supply (UPS) for sensitive electronics.

Strengths

  • Extremely lightweight (1.8 kg) and compact palm-sized housing with an ergonomic handle
  • Long-lasting LiFePO4 battery chemistry delivers over 3,000 charge cycles to 80% capacity
  • Integrated 10ms UPS mode ensures seamless backup for routers, cameras, and gaming devices during power cuts
  • Fast 150W AC recharging refuels the unit to 100% in just 70 minutes
  • Versatile 6-outlet array includes a 200W AC socket, 100W USB-C fast charging, USB-A ports, and DC output
  • Includes a 3-mode built-in LED light (cool, warm, SOS) and Bluetooth app connectivity

Trade-offs

  • 128Wh capacity is optimized for smaller electronics and short trips rather than heavy appliances
  • AC output is capped at 200W continuous (300W peak), so high-wattage heating devices cannot be run

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 150W turbo AC input for a full recharge in 70 minutes, alongside 100W max solar input (12–28V) and 100W car charging via cigarette lighter.

Frequently asked questions

How does the UPS function work?

It features a built-in 10ms switchover time. If grid power fails, it instantly switches to battery power to keep sensitive devices like routers and mini PCs running without interruption.

What devices can the 200W AC outlet power?

It can easily power laptops, drones, cameras, small portable monitors, LED lights, and low-wattage camping electronics under 200W.
