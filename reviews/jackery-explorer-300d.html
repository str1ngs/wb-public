The Jackery Explorer 300D is an ultra-portable 288Wh power station weighing just 2.5 kg with a slim portrait design. Featuring a built-in 140W USB-C cable that doubles as a carry handle, dual 140W USB-C ports, and exceptional energy efficiency, it delivers reliable off-grid power ideal for hiking, camping, and running mobile devices or projectors.

Who it's for

Hikers, outdoor enthusiasts, campers, and minimalist travelers who rely exclusively on solar power and need an exceptionally lightweight, highly efficient power station for small electronics, cameras, and projectors.

Strengths

  • Extremely lightweight and compact at just 2.5 kg
  • Reliable low-wattage solar charging startup that kicks in automatically early in the morning and handles trickle inputs down to 6W
  • Integrated 140W USB-C cable doubles as a convenient carry handle
  • Long-lasting LiFePO4 battery rated for up to 4,000 charge cycles (10 years of use)

Trade-offs

  • Small 288Wh base capacity is built for portability rather than powering heavy household appliances
  • Lacks traditional AC outlet ports in favor of versatile DC and USB-C output configurations
  • Relies primarily on DC/USB-C charging methods rather than standard heavy wall-brick AC inputs

Charging and expansion

Designed specifically for solar and flexible DC charging, the Explorer 300D excels at low-light solar harvesting, automatically initiating charge sequences in the early morning even at low trickle inputs like 6W. Combined charging configurations can drive the unit to 80% capacity in approximately 70 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What devices can I plug into the Explorer 300D?

It features 5 outputs including two 140W USB-C ports, a 65W USB-C port, a 15W USB-A port, and a 12V car port, making it ideal for smartphones, laptops, drones, cameras, and mobile projectors.

Is the unit heavy or difficult to carry?

Not at all. Weighing only 2.5 kg with integrated 140W cable that doubles as a carry handle, it is built for ultimate portability.
