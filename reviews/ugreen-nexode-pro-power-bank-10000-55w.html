The UGREEN Nexode Pro Power Bank (10,000mAh, 55W) utilizes exclusive AirPyra technology to pack a 36Wh capacity into an ultra-lightweight 249g body. Featuring a built-in 22cm Type-C cable, 55W Xiaomi Mi Turbo charging, and 45W Power Delivery fast charge support, it delivers high performance in a pocket-friendly form factor.

Who it's for

Daily commuters, smartphone users, and travelers seeking a lightweight, pocket-sized power bank with built-in cabling and specialized fast-charging protocols for Xiaomi and iPhone.

Strengths

  • Ultra-compact and lightweight at only 249g thanks to advanced AirPyra technology
  • Supports 55W Xiaomi Mi Turbo charging to refuel compatible phones rapidly
  • Delivers 45W PD fast charging to power iPhones, iPads, and Galaxy devices
  • Includes a convenient 22 cm integrated Type-C cable alongside extra USB-C and USB-A ports
  • Features a dedicated trickle-charge mode for low-power accessories like wireless earbuds

Trade-offs

  • 10,000mAh (36Wh) capacity is limited to about 1 to 2 full smartphone charges
  • Pass-through charging is restricted to standard 5V speeds

Charging and expansion

Supports up to 45W fast input via USB-C, allowing a full recharge in approximately 1.5 hours using a high-output wall adapter.

Frequently asked questions

Does it come with a built-in cable?

Yes, it features an integrated 22 cm Type-C cable for instant, cable-free charging.

Can it fast-charge Xiaomi and iPhone devices?

Yes, it supports 55W Mi Turbo charging for compatible Xiaomi models and 45W Power Delivery for iPhones and other modern smartphones.

How do I charge small accessories like earbuds?

You can activate the trickle-charge mode by holding down the power button for 3 seconds.
