The Anker Laptop Power Bank (25K, 165W) is a heavy-duty portable charger that bridges the gap between pocket-sized convenience and workstation-grade power. Featuring a massive 25,000mAh (approx. 90Wh) capacity and a 165W max output across built-in and retractable cables, it delivers serious juice for power-hungry laptops and accessories without crossing airline carry-on limits.

Who it's for

Digital nomads, remote workers, and content creators who need to fast-charge high-draw laptops and multiple devices simultaneously away from a wall outlet.

Strengths

  • Easy to carry
  • Retractable cables included
  • It can refuel up to 30% capacity in just 20 minutes using a 100W USB-C input source
  • Aircraft-compatible

Trade-offs

  • Relatively expensive

Charging and expansion

Equipped with a 100W max USB-C port/cable and a total combined output of 165W Max. It supports a wide voltage/amperage spread (up to 20V/5A) for rapid recharging and multi-device distribution.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to recharge the power bank?

Using a 100W or higher wall charger at room temperature will take approximately 2 hours to fully recharge, or roughly 20 minutes to reach 30%.
