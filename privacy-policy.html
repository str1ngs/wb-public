What we process, and on what legal basis

Theme preference (light/dark mode): stored only in your browser's localStorage (key wattbench-theme). It never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us — this isn't personal data processing on our end at all.

Google Fonts: this site currently loads fonts from Google's CDN, which sends your browser's IP address to Google (a data transfer outside the EU) for each page load. If you're adapting this template for production, consider self-hosting the font files instead — this avoids the transfer entirely and is the approach EU data protection authorities generally recommend.

Analytics: none are active by default. If you add analytics (e.g. a cookieless tool like Plausible, or Google Analytics) or ad scripts, you must update this section, identify the legal basis (typically consent under the ePrivacy Directive for anything non-essential), and add a consent mechanism before enabling them.

Your rights under GDPR

Because this site doesn't collect personal data server-side, most GDPR data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) have nothing to act on here.
