What are affiliate links?

Some links on this site — including the “Check on Amazon” and “Check on [Brand]” buttons on review pages — are affiliate links. If you click through and make a purchase, WattBench may earn a commission from the retailer or manufacturer. This comes at no additional cost to you; the price you pay is the same either way.

What this doesn't affect
