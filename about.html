About WattBench

WattBench (wattbench.eu) exists because most power device reviews read like the manufacturer wrote them. We wanted a site that leads with the spec sheet — real capacity, real output, real charge times — and only then tells you what it's actually like to own one.

How we score

Every unit we cover gets five sub-scores out of 5: value, power, portability, charge speed, and reliability. Those are weighted and averaged into the overall score you see on every review and in the comparison tables. The same rubric applies to every brand, and no manufacturer can pay to move up it.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links — if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects which products we cover or how we score them. See our affiliate disclosure for the full policy.

Corrections

Specs change, prices change, and manufacturers update firmware and hardware without much notice. If you spot something out of date, that's on us to catch and correct as part of our review process.
